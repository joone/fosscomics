3. The Era of Commercial Computers

Jun 25, 2019
In 1946, ENIAC and EDVAC engineers J. Presper Eckert and John Mauchly founded a company that became the Eckert-Mauchly Computer Corporation and developed UNIVAC I. During the 1950s, IBM introduced computers including the 701, 704, and mass-produced 650, while Fortran and Lisp helped expand the possibilities of programming. This marked the beginning of widespread commercial computing.
