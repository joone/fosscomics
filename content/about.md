F/OSS Comics is an illustrated history of computing and Free and Open Source Software. The series follows the path from early computers and software engineering through hacker culture, Unix, and the free and open source movements that followed.

Each episode uses short dialogue and simple drawings to make technical history approachable without losing the details that make it interesting. The comics are written for technically curious readers; no specialist background is required.

The series is written and illustrated by [Joone Hur](https://joone.net), an open source developer and comic artist. It was originally published in Korean, and this English edition translates and revises the original comics for a wider audience.

I do my best to keep the history and technical explanations accurate and to include references where they are useful. If you find an error or have a suggestion, please leave a comment on the relevant comic or submit an issue or pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/joone/fosscomics).

Individual comic images are often shared on their own. If you share one, please use it as-is without editing or cropping it, and link back to [fosscomics.com](https://fosscomics.com) if possible. You can also follow [@fosscomics](https://twitter.com/fosscomics) for project updates.

F/OSS Comics is an independent project. Sponsorship helps cover the domain, drawing equipment, and reference books. You can support the project through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/joone).

Thank you!
