About

ENKO

F/OSS Comics is a comic series about the history of computing and free and open source software. It covers early computers, software engineering, hacker culture, Unix, and the free software and open source movements, telling the stories of the people involved and how their projects began.

I'm Joone Hur. I write and draw F/OSS Comics, and I'm also an open source developer, contributing mainly to Chromium and WebKit.

The original Korean series is complete. The first ten episodes of the English edition were published on Medium. I'm now expanding the stories and redrawing much of the artwork. Each revised episode is published here in both Korean and English.

The comics are based on real events, and I try to get the history and technical details right. The dialogue is written for the comic and does not reproduce the actual conversations. If you find an error or have a suggestion, please leave a comment on the episode.

You're welcome to share the comics on social media with credit to F/OSS Comics. To share individual images, please use the site's built-in sharing feature and keep the artwork intact. I'd appreciate a link back to fosscomics.com, too. For other uses of the images, please contact me. You can follow @fosscomics for updates.

F/OSS Comics is a personal project. If you'd like to support it, you can do so through GitHub Sponsors. Contributions help pay for the domain, drawing equipment, reference books, and hosting for the Korean site.

Thank you!
